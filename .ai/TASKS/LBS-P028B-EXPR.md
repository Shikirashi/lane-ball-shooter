## Verified methodological defect

In `scripts/analysis/threat_run_selector.gd:choose()`, `maximum_urgency` is computed across **all four lanes**. When `maximum_urgency > 0` but **no remaining legal color in the current four-run bag has positive urgency** (the endangered lane color is already consumed), the code still draws from its own `_rng` and uniformly reshuffles remaining colors, despite **having zero threat-relevant preference**. This creates stochastic differences in the adaptive arms completely unrelated to beneficial threat-aware behavior and contaminates paired scenario deltas.

This is **demonstrated by the committed synthetic report**: in `urgent_exhausted` most of the 51,099 world-threat selections per arm had unavailable urgent colors (50,599); yet mild/moderate/strong show ~**46% intervention rates** when they cannot help the threatened color. The only ~500 legal cases are principally the first selections. Those substitutions are not targeted rescue; they are arbitrary reshuffles relative to the production baseline. The primary P028B-EXP report's conclusions (moderate improving total starvation but hurting YELLOW) must be treated **provisional** until retested.

Still observe/report that the globally most threatened color is exhausted and that a world threat existed.
