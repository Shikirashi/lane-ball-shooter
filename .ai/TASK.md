## First principle: topology-based support and motion, not gap-reason motors

Authoritative ordered rigid segments from P011 remain the foundation: each segment contains stable ball UIDs/colors, with one anchor + fixed `spacing=58` centers; snapshot/render from that model. In RIGHT-to-LEFT screen order, a segment may have contact/support immediately to its RIGHT, or may have a **real spatial gap to its RIGHT**.

At every gameplay substep:
- The virtual offscreen-right feeder/pusher drives its **currently connected rightmost train** LEFT at `ChainModel.DEFAULT_SPEED=34.0 px/s`. The infinite source is NOT a finite level-defined ball queue; continue fresh and FIFO arrivals forever while the match is active.
- A connected segment **with no support immediately on its RIGHT** (a gap separating it from the pusher-connected train) is UNSUPPORTED, regardless of whether the gap originally arose from a player clear, insertion, rollover, or FEED staging. It accelerates RIGHT under virtual gravity, together as one rigid block, until it physically touches the next segment to its right. It does **not** continue left forever at -34 merely because a gap is labeled FEED.
- Supported/connected segments move LEFT under the virtual pusher; don't model per-ball checking, per-ball forces, bounce, friction or spring physics.
- On reaching exact contact at one `spacing`, clamp, merge the segment lists and propagate **support/pusher attachment** through the newly connected train. No overlap, jitter, oscillation or positional snapping through other balls.
- Distinguish a **causal cascade authorization token** from support/motion. FEED versus CAUSAL may remain as metadata ONLY to decide whether a physical reconnection can cause a match/cascade; they must NOT prevent gravity from closing a real gap. A player-caused match opens an authorized boundary, with cascade eligible on later matching-end contact; a naturally formed/feed gap closes physically but does not auto-clear a naturally occurring long same-color run. Propagate/retire causal eligibility correctly through rapid consecutive matches and segment merges.
- Prefer explicit support propagation from right to left plus a single constrained segment integrator, O(number of segments) per bounded substep. This is **one** motion algorithm rather than competing FEED and CAUSAL motors. Remove or disable old `_integrate_segments` CAUSAL-only rollback branch and old no-motor FEED behavior. Don't fake movement in `BallChainView`.

### Gravity dynamics and animation
- Use one positive RIGHTWARD velocity state per unsupported **segment/connected rigid group**, not per ball; configurable `GRAVITY_ACCELERATION` initial tune 900 px/s² and `GRAVITY_MAX_RIGHT_SPEED` initial tune 420 px/s. To preserve the established satisfying recoil, initial unsupported speed may be ~60 px/s (previous P010 behavior) if tested. Choose a consistent **net world-space** velocity convention and document whether the baseline -34 is included or overridden. An unsupported segment must be seen to move RIGHT after a short interval and its members must move exactly together.
- Supported segments move -34 px/s; a freely reversing segment never gets forcibly carried left by an unrelated incoming train. Multiple independent gaps can close simultaneously; no two distinct motors may move the same rigid member twice.
- If a segment's immediate-right neighbor is also unsupported, let it close its own right gap first / solve physically by nearest-right constraint. Gravity is towards right even for FEED-created gaps; don't accelerate incoming freshly staged balls left after visibility. The currently rightmost source-driven segment always remains pushed left; source attachment transfers deterministically on legal right-edge admission. Avoid oscillation due to repeated rightmost FEED admissions.
- Do not permit ball overlap in **logical OR rendered** centers. Existing P011 P012 spacing guarantees apply after each microstep: IDs unique, strict X ordering; normal internal spacing 58; physical diameter 44; right-edge clipped view consistent. Preserve event-time ordering and frame-rate independence for 30/60/120 FPS and larger deltas.
- A ball clearing near the LEFT exit may leave no front segment: no ghost rollback. Unconnected right-edge staged balls cannot create unbounded backlog. If the entire chain clears, new supply still emerges from fully offscreen RIGHT promptly.
- `F3` should expose number of unsupported segments, number of gaps awaiting gravitational closure, min logical/render spacing and invariant violations, and useful bounded support/velocity samples. Eliminate obsolete MC/MF diagnosis if it no longer describes movement.

## Second principle: FINITE enemies per encounter, INFINITE balls until encounter ends

Current `LaneCombatModel._update_spawning` unconditionally repeats as long as `auto_spawn` and slot availability allow. Replace this endless enemy source with a deterministic **finite per-lane spawn budget**.

- Initial prototype tuning: **8 total enemies PER lane** (4 lanes => 32 total for one encounter), including each lane's one pre-spawned initial enemy. Expose `DEFAULT_ENEMIES_PER_LANE=8` in one obvious tunable configuration; document rationale and allow changing for balancing. This is a first playable **single encounter/wave**, not eight simultaneously on screen and not an arbitrary runtime kill counter. Later levels/waves are outside P015 scope.
- Each lane maintains `enemy_spawn_budget`, `spawned_this_encounter` / `remaining_to_spawn`, `total_killed`, `total_emergency_cleared`, `active_enemy_count`. `max_enemies=5` is simultaneous on-screen cap, not total budget. Initial spawn consumes one slot; manual/test spawns must have clearly documented accounting semantics. If at max simultaneous capacity, defer (do NOT consume) a budget slot until a valid spawn succeeds. Subsequent timer ticks never spawn beyond total budget. Setters and reset restore deterministic budget.
- Enemy eliminations by tier-1/2 projectiles, tier-3 two-adjacent-lane wipe, and the lane's one-time emergency discharge ALL count as neutralized (or make defeated counter explicit); they must not accidentally replenish the spawn budget. If a living enemy breaches/causes terminal loss, existing loss precedence holds; no simultaneous win. Preserve defender ACTIVE/DOWN/revival, HP=2, tier2 one-hit damage=2, tier3 two-lane wipe, hop-only 0.24 unit/s, attack SFX/visuals.
- **Victory condition:** after all four lanes have emitted their COMPLETE finite spawn quotas and there are **zero remaining live enemies** anywhere, and NO pending terminal breach/gameover, the player **wins this encounter**. This is a victory by successfully defeating/neutralizing all scheduled enemies (including emergency clearing), NOT by removing all conveyor balls. Check only when Battlefield is fully initialized and after combat updates/kill events, and don't mistakenly declare victory because the scene is momentarily empty between spawns. Do not require active defender projectiles to disappear before winning; pending enemy count matters.
- Add a lightweight `VICTORY` or `WAVE CLEARED` overlay reusing the current gameover overlay/layout style, final score, and `RESTART [R]`. A terminal win freezes combat/chain/projectiles/input just as terminal loss does; restarting resets spawn budgets, win/lose status, chain, cooldown, score, RNGs, VFX/SFX and defender charges. Defeat by breach or all defenders DOWN (if implemented elsewhere) must take precedence over victory when both conditions occur within the same update. Avoid two contradictory terminal signals.
- HUD may show compact progress e.g. `ENEMIES 12/32` defeated/neutralized (without interfering with upper-right swap button, combo and score), or a small progress bar; a stat that tracks remaining live+unspawned is acceptable if clear. Do not mistake 5 max concurrent enemies for the wave budget. Preserve Web/mobile responsive safe area.
- No new enemy types, no wave selector, no post-victory endless respawn, no automatic transition to infinitely many enemy waves, no loot or unrelated balance overhaul. This is a fixed encounter objective with infinite balls until victory/defeat; player can restart to replay.
- Projectile collisions/spawn ordering and terminal state must be deterministic under update delta and with tier3 instant clear, large deltas and test-mode manual spawning.
