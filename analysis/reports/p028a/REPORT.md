# LBS-P028A deterministic balancing laboratory

Generated by Godot `4.7.2-stable (official)` in 7.13 seconds. This is an **analysis-only** artifact: no production speed, colors, spawning, HP, controls, or weights were changed.

## Reproduction and provenance

```bash
./scripts/analyze_balance.sh --seeds 500 --seed-start 28001 --duration 120.0 --step 0.100 --output analysis/reports/p028a
```

The generator sweep uses paired source seeds `28001..28500`, 300 fresh balls per seed (150000 total per weight set). Each source seed expands through the documented `seed_tuple` into independent chain, magazine, spawn, attack and Snake seeds. The scenario table contains 25 bounded real-model cases.

## Definitions and fidelity

- **Starter** is the fixed 12-ball `SEED_PATTERN`, reported separately from weighted fresh generation.
- **Visible** means a physical ball circle intersects `[0, lane_width]`; retained off-left history and clipped right feed do not count as available.
- **Match opportunity** is a visible, physically adjacent same-color pair. Bots aim at that pair and call the real `try_insert_shot()` after 0.20 s; only actual `match_completed` events feed real `MatchProgression` and `Battlefield.handle_match`. Pre-existing triples never auto-pop.
- **Usable** additionally requires that color loaded/next and the 0.4 s cooldown ready. **Threat starvation** measures an enemy estimated within 10 s of its defender while one of those conditions is missing.
- Hopper time-to-danger uses its authoritative average movement speed; Snake stun/transfer delay is included; stationary Healyzards have no direct arrival time. This remains an estimate.

`observe_only` deliberately never fires and is expected to lose; it isolates physical visibility and enemy arrival. `basic_matcher` chooses loaded before next and swaps only when next has a visible pair. `threat_priority` uses the same legal actions but prefers the loaded/next color with lowest estimated time-to-danger. These deterministic policies are measurement instruments, not human-skill models.

## Worked worst threatened-lane trace

Seed `28001`, `basic_matcher`, speed 102, `baseline` weights: RED lane remained non-usable for 7.30s at worst. Aggregate attribution in that run was chain geometry `7.30s`, ammo `0.00s`, cooldown/flight timing `0.10s`; that lane's loaded-or-next availability was 72.8% and its longest adjacent-pair drought was 12.60s. This trace identifies the causal bottleneck without claiming the bot made human-optimal choices.

## Findings and P028B recommendations

1. **GREEN generation:** GREEN is not structurally underweighted. Compare its visible occupancy, pair drought and threat-starvation columns before attributing the anecdote to generator probability; the data separates generator balance from short-window/geometry/ammo effects.
2. **Long runs:** use the run-vs-ball shares above. A small run percentage can still dominate generated balls and perceived clusters. The experimental weights are the cleanest P028B candidate because they target cluster shape without color favoritism.
3. **Speed:** in the paired two-seed sensitivity set, baseline GREEN pair drought fell from 11.85s at 102 to 10.00s at 120 and 8.70s at 140, with 2/2 victories at each speed, although mean GREEN occupancy also fell. That supports phone A/B testing 120 and 140; 160 is a sensitivity bound, not a recommendation. Automated outcomes alone cannot establish fun or touch readability.
4. **P028B experiment:** first A/B the exploratory run weights at 102/120/140 with the same seeds and human phone sessions. At 102 they reduced paired GREEN pair drought from 11.85s to 8.85s, but only two combat seeds were used. If threat starvation remains color-specific after controlling geometry and ammo, test a soft recent-ball deficit cap; do not guarantee rescue colors or use raw enemy distance.

## Limitations

The lab uses authentic models but not `Main`'s rendered input/SFX loop. It explicitly reproduces shot cooldown/flight and production logical update order; its aim heuristic only recognizes adjacent pairs, does not search multi-shot setup trees, and cannot model human aiming errors, perception, touch occlusion or strategic emergency timing. Time-to-danger approximates Hopper hopping by average speed and treats Healyzard as pressure through healing rather than a direct breach clock. Scenario counts are deliberately bounded, so tails in combat outcomes are descriptive rather than population estimates. Android device playtesting remains required.
