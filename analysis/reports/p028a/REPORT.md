# LBS-P028AR corrected deterministic balancing laboratory

Generated by Godot `4.7.2-stable (official)` in 8.03 seconds. P028AR removes the original lab's impossible target-UID homing: every bot shot now keeps one immutable launch X for its complete 0.20-second flight. This is an **analysis-only** artifact; no production speed, colors, spawning, HP, controls, or weights were changed.

## Reproduction and provenance

```bash
./scripts/analyze_balance.sh --seeds 500 --seed-start 28001 --duration 120.0 --step 0.100 --output analysis/reports/p028a
```

The generator sweep uses paired source seeds `28001..28500`, 300 fresh balls per seed (150000 total per weight set). Each source seed expands through the documented `seed_tuple` into independent chain, magazine, spawn, attack and Snake seeds. The scenario table contains 25 bounded real-model cases.

## Definitions and fidelity

- **Starter** is the fixed 12-ball `SEED_PATTERN`, reported separately from weighted fresh generation.
- **Visible** means a physical ball circle intersects `[0, lane_width]`; retained off-left history and clipped right feed do not count as available.
- **Launch opportunity** is a visible, physically adjacent same-color pair plus loaded/next ammo and a ready 0.4-second cooldown. It is a proxy, **not a guaranteed hit or match**.
- At firing, both matcher policies store the selected left ball's current X minus one pixel, clamped to `[0, lane_width]`. That X never changes. The chain advances precisely to the 0.20-second arrival boundary before the real `try_insert_shot()` collision; a reporting `target_uid` cannot affect collision. No predictive lead is used. Gravity, speed, another ball crossing the axis or target removal may cause a miss or off-target insertion.
- Only actual `match_completed` events feed real `MatchProgression` and `Battlefield.handle_match`; pre-existing triples never auto-pop. **Threat starvation** measures an enemy estimated within 10 s of its defender while a launch opportunity is missing.
- Hopper time-to-danger uses its authoritative average movement speed; Snake stun/transfer delay is included; stationary Healyzards have no direct arrival time. This remains an estimate.

`observe_only` deliberately never fires and is expected to lose; its 0% hit rate means no arrivals, not failed aim. `basic_matcher` chooses loaded before next and swaps only when next has a visible pair. `threat_priority` uses the same legal actions but prefers the loaded/next color with lowest estimated time-to-danger. Both freeze current X at launch. JSON/CSV distinguish original-target contact from another current ball legitimately crossing the fixed axis. These deterministic policies are measurement instruments, not human-skill models.

## Worked worst threatened-lane trace

Seed `28002`, `basic_matcher`, speed 102, `baseline` weights: BLUE lane remained non-usable for 5.00s at worst. Aggregate attribution in that run was chain geometry `5.00s`, ammo `11.90s`, cooldown/flight timing `1.50s`; that lane's loaded-or-next availability was 71.4% and its longest adjacent-pair drought was 11.20s. This trace identifies the causal bottleneck without claiming the bot made human-optimal choices.

## Findings and P028B recommendations

1. **GREEN generation:** GREEN is not structurally underweighted. The 500-seed generator figures are unchanged by P028AR, but generation equality does not prove visible or human-usable equality. Pair availability is only a launch proxy and corrected shots can miss.
2. **Long runs:** use the run-vs-ball shares above. A small run percentage can still dominate generated balls and perceived clusters. The experimental weights are the cleanest P028B candidate because they target cluster shape without color favoritism.
3. **Speed:** in the paired two-seed baseline set, GREEN pair drought fell from 11.45s at 102 to 10.95s at 120 and 9.45s at 140, but fixed-X hit rate also fell from 88.5% to 87.0% and 82.8%. All 2/2 cases still won at every speed, demonstrating that this bot remains too strong and the sample too small—not that acceleration is safe. Faster motion tightens human touch timing, so no live speed increase is recommended from these data alone.
4. **P028B experiment:** exploratory weights at 102 reduced paired GREEN pair drought from 11.45s to 8.75s but also reduced hit rate from 88.5% to 82.1%; only two combat seeds were used. Human phone A/B should precede any run-weight or speed decision. If threat starvation remains color-specific after controlling geometry and ammo, separately test a soft recent-ball deficit cap without guaranteed rescue colors.

## Limitations

The lab uses authentic models but not `Main`'s rendered input/SFX loop. Fixed-X timing and collision now match the playable rule, but the bot selects exact pair centers instantly at decision samples; it has no finger error, perception delay, touch occlusion, predictive lead, multi-shot setup search or strategic emergency timing. An insertion may legitimately hit a different ball crossing the axis, and not every insertion matches. Time-to-danger approximates Hopper hopping by average speed and treats Healyzard as pressure through healing rather than a direct breach clock. All matcher scenarios still won, confirming bot win rate cannot establish human difficulty. Scenario sensitivity cells contain only two seeds, so tails are descriptive rather than population estimates. Android device playtesting remains required.
